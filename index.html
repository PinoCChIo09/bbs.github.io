Centuries ago, the Deepborn were banished beneath the earth. Now the ancient seals are crumbling — and the creatures are back.

They're strange, they're stubborn, and there are far too many of them. Your job is simple: build a line of defense, make every coin count and keep the horde away from your castle.

Place defenses around the path, survive increasingly demanding waves and protect your stronghold.

Who we are

Bond Breaker Studios

Our vision

Bond Breaker Studios began with three friends, a shared love for games and the idea of creating something of our own. We are an independent team building worlds, characters and gameplay experiences together.

Our upcoming project is a mobile tower-defense game that brings together approachable strategy, playful monster designs and a fantasy world built around defending your castle.
